About

Hi, I'm Shrmurda, a community builder and growth marketer who's spent the last few years in the trenches of Web3: Discord servers at 2am, X spaces during a mint, and the quiet work of getting strangers to trust a project enough to stay. I'm so passionate about building a solid community that it became the whole point of the work, not a side effect of it.

That passion led me to found Halocreators, where I help projects turn scattered followers into people who actually show up and to spend just as much energy teaching newcomers how to spot the scams that target growing communities before those scams cost them anything. With Halocreators, I've been able to onboard lots of newbies and guide them through their creator journey.

What I focus on

Community strategy and moderation · Web3 growth marketing and campaign execution · NFT and token launch support · Scam awareness and member education

Case study

Magritte Checks × gillesdc

$89K in trading volume driven on OpenSea

When gillesdc, a talented artist, was about to launch Magritte Checks — a collection reimagining René Magritte's surrealist motifs through Jack Butcher's checks format — I led the marketing push around the drop.

The campaign helped carry the collection to $89K in trading volume on OpenSea, turning a niche art crossover into a collection people actively wanted to own.

Growth I report is growth I can point to — real members, real volume, not follower counts that vanish after a giveaway.
